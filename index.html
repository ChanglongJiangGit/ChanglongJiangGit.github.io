Changlong Jiang 姜昌龙

I am a Ph.D. student at the Department of Artificial Intelligence and Automation, Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST), advised by Prof. Yang Xiao. Before that, I received my bachelor's degree from HUST. I was born in Tai'an, Shandong, a beautiful historical and cultural city at the foot of Mount Tai.

My research interests include computer vision and pattern recognition. Currently, I mainly focus on Pose Estimation, Embodied AI, Hand-Object Interaction, Hand Motion Generation, and Hand Pose Tracking.
